How to connect a channel
Before connecting a channel to Buffer, log in to the correct social account in another browser tab. This helps us identify the account you want to connect, especially if you manage multiple accounts on the same platform.
Steps:
Open a new browser tab and log in to the exact social account you want to connect (for example, log in at
facebook.com
as the profile that is an admin of the Page, or log in to the specific TikTok account).
Open a second tab in the same browser, log in to Buffer, click
+ New
in the top navigation bar, and select
Connect a New Channel
. (If you already have channels connected, click
Connect New Channel
, then choose the network.)
Click the social network you want to connect to and follow the network's prompts to grant Buffer permission.
When the network asks what to allow,
approve everything it offers
(see the important note below), then finish. The channel then appears in your channel list.
To reorder channels in the channel menu, click and drag a channel to your preferred position. By default, new channels appear newest first in your channel list. Once you click and drag channels into your preferred order, Buffer remembers that order.

Connecting more than one account from the same network
You can connect multiple accounts from the same social network, such as two Instagram accounts or three TikTok accounts. Buffer connects the account currently logged in to your browser, and no account picker appears during the connection process. If Buffer shows an
“already connected”
message or only displays another account, follow these steps to connect the correct one:
Log out of the social channel completely, or use an incognito/private window.
In the incognito window, log in to the account you want to connect to Buffer. This prevents Buffer from connecting to the account that is already signed in on your regular browser.
Log in to the correct social account first in one browser tab, then connect to it in Buffer from a second tab.
This allows us to quickly detect the channel you want to connect to.
:::info
Facebook and LinkedIn work a little differently: you always authorize through your
personal profile
, and Buffer then shows the Pages you manage so you can pick the right one. Signing in with your personal profile is normal and does
not
connect or post to that personal profile.
:::
What access do I need before connecting a social channel?
If a social account isn't listed or the Page doesn't appear during the connection, you may be signed in to the wrong account or lack the required role. Check the requirements for each network below.
Facebook Pages:
Sign in with a Facebook profile that has
Full control
of the Page. If the Page is missing, confirm that your profile has Full control and that all Pages were selected under
Choose what you allow
.
LinkedIn Pages:
You must be a
Super Admin
of the LinkedIn Page. Sign in with your personal LinkedIn profile, then select a Page you’re a Super Admin of. Content Admins and other roles cannot connect Pages to Buffer.
YouTube:
You must be signed in as the channel
Owner
, not a Manager. Log out of all Google accounts, then sign in only to the Google account that owns the channel. Using an incognito window can help. For Brand Account channels, sign in with the owning Google account. Newly created YouTube channels may need to wait a day or two before they can be connected.
Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Substack, and X/Twitter.
Be signed in to the exact account you want to connect before you start. Follow the instructions in
Connecting more than one account from the same network
if you manage multiple accounts.
FAQ
Once I connect a channel, do I need to stay logged in to that social account to post?
No. When you connect a channel, the social network gives Buffer an
access token
, which is like a secure key to access that account. As long as your login and permissions stay valid, Buffer uses that token to post on your behalf, so you don't have to stay logged in to the social account in your browser.
Can I connect multiple accounts from the same social network to Buffer?
Yes. Because Buffer connects whichever account is signed in in your browser, log in to the exact account first (or use an incognito window), then connect. For Facebook Pages, sign in as a Page admin; for LinkedIn Pages, sign in as a Super Admin; for YouTube, sign in as the channel owner.
Which social networks are unsupported?
We don’t currently have an integration with Snapchat, Reddit, WhatsApp, Telegram, Twitch, OnlyFans, or lemon8, so these can't be connected to Buffer. You can check the table at the top of this article for the channels we do support. If you'd like to see another channel added, you can log your interest on our
Suggestions Board
.
Can I schedule posts to Discord?
No, unfortunately, it's not possible to schedule posts to Discord. However, you can
join our community on Discord here
.
